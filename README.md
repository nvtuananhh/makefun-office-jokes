## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one funny
line about office life to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I declined a meeting to finish the work from the last meeting, and got invited to a meeting about why I declined the meeting.

The line is 125 characters long. The joke lands on the judge's brief:
it is squarely about office life (the meeting that exists to discuss
the meeting you skipped), it is a real joke with a turn rather than a
mild observation, it is fresh — no Monday-morning or photocopier line —
it is self-contained, and the twist is packed into the last few words,
with the decline itself needing its own meeting.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Output

Writes one line (125 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that is set).
